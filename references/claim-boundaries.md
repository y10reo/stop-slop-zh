# Claim Boundaries

Use this file when a text makes market, product, investment, academic, legal, or causal claims. The fix is not to weaken every sentence. The fix is to keep the conclusion inside the evidence.

## Rule

Before rewriting a conclusion, ask:

- What is the evidence type: official claim, third-party report, experiment, user review, crowdfunding data, financial metric, legal rule, or author's inference?
- What does it directly measure?
- What does it not measure?
- Does the sentence turn correlation, pledge, plan, or self-description into proof?

If the evidence is weak, keep the claim narrow and attribute it.

## Evidence Limits

| Evidence | Can support | Cannot support |
|---|---|---|
| Crowdfunding amount/backers | Early interest, pledge behavior, launch attention, niche backer willingness to pay | Product efficacy, mass-market demand, retention, profitability, delivery capability |
| Product page claims | What the company says or promises | That the feature works, is certified, or beats competitors |
| User reviews | Reported experience from that review group | Whole-market satisfaction or objective medical effect |
| Market-size report | Category size, growth estimate, macro context | A specific startup's demand, conversion, or defensibility |
| Technical datasheet | Component specs under stated conditions | Full-system performance unless the assumptions are modeled |
| Theoretical model | Plausible risk, constraint, or estimate | Final proof without test data |
| Media coverage | Public attention and narrative | Industry consensus or commercial success |
| Legal/regulatory text | Compliance requirement or legal risk | Certain enforcement outcome without facts and legal analysis |

## Safer Claim Patterns

Use these shapes:

- "这说明..." only when the evidence directly measures the claim.
- "至少可以说明..." for narrow inference.
- "更稳妥的判断是..." when correcting overclaim.
- "从现有材料看..." when source coverage is incomplete.
- "项目方宣称..." for unverified company claims.
- "该数据支持早期兴趣，但不能证明..." when a sentence needs a caveat.

Avoid these unless proven:

- 成功验证了...
- 充分证明...
- 强烈需求
- 市场真实需求
- 必将迎来...
- 唯一利器
- 最佳投资切入点
- 极低获客成本
- 风险彻底封锁

## Product And Crowdfunding

Weak:

> 48.2 万美元众筹金额成功验证了产品的市场需求。

Better:

> 48.2 万美元众筹金额说明，已有一批早期用户愿意为这类睡眠耳机提前付费。它还不能证明大众市场接受度、复购率或产品交付能力。

Weak:

> 该产品已被验证具备获客能力。

Better:

> 该产品在两个海外众筹平台上获得了早期曝光和付费支持。后续能否在主流电商、线下渠道或复购场景中获客，还需要单独验证。

## Investment And Due Diligence

Venture reports can keep judgment, but the judgment must show its source and confidence level.

Prefer:

- "投委会可以把它列为核心风险"
- "现有资料支持有条件推进"
- "应以分期付款和工程测试作为交割前提"
- "理论模型提示续航存在重大偏差"

Avoid:

- "彻底封锁风险"
- "唯一利器"
- "完美展现"
- "绝对前提"
- "必然成为优质标的"

## Academic And Report Writing

Academic/report prose may keep structure and cautious terms. Remove unsupported elevation:

Weak:

> 本研究具有重要理论意义和实践价值。

Better:

> 本报告用公开行业报告和元器件规格书搭建了一个模拟尽调框架，但缺少样机实测，结论只能作为课程场景下的投资推演。

## Legal And Contract Clauses

Do not simplify legal terms if precision would be lost. Tighten only rhetorical overstatement:

Weak:

> 仲裁裁决在法律上对争议各方均具有不可推翻的强制约束力。

Better:

> 仲裁裁决为终局裁决，对各方具有法律约束力。
