# Scenarios

Choose the edit strength by genre. The goal is not to make all Chinese text casual. The goal is to remove AI traces while preserving the job the text must do.

## General Article / Blog / Zhihu

Hard remove:

- 总分总 skeleton
- 三件套排比
- 段尾金句
- 空泛背景段
- "这背后反映的是..."

Keep:

- Author's stance
- Useful examples
- Necessary definitions

Prefer:

- A concrete opening scene
- A direct claim
- One argument developed well

## Xiaohongshu / Douyin / WeChat Social Copy

Hard remove:

- Fake intimacy copied from platform templates
- Over-stacked emojis
- "闭眼冲、天花板、狠狠爱住" when not natural for the author
- Generic hooks such as "一定要看到最后"

Keep:

- Platform rhythm when the user wants platform-native copy
- Short lines if they help scanning
- Some emotional words if the product or story needs them

Prefer:

- Specific experience details
- Visible before/after
- Plain recommendation boundaries: who should buy, who should skip

## Product Copy

Hard remove:

- "赋能、无缝、极致、强大、全新升级、开启新篇章"
- Unsupported "领先、首创、革命性"
- Feature piles without user tasks

Keep:

- Benefits
- Feature names
- Brand tone

Prefer:

- User task -> feature -> evidence
- Specific constraints: time saved, fewer steps, lower error rate, compatible systems

## Work Message / Email

Hard remove:

- Over-polite padding
- Long background before the ask
- "百忙之中、烦请知悉、望周知" unless required by workplace norm

Keep:

- Politeness
- Ownership
- Deadline
- Context needed for action

Prefer:

- First sentence states the ask or update.
- Dates, owners, next steps are explicit.

## Report / Briefing / Summary

Hard remove:

- Empty executive conclusions
- Vague attribution
- Decorative strategy language
- "挑战与机遇并存" unless followed by concrete facts

Keep:

- Sectioning
- Risk language
- Cautious claims

Prefer:

- Decision relevance
- Evidence source
- Clear distinction between fact, estimate, and judgment

## Venture / Due Diligence / Investment Memo

Hard remove:

- Heroic investor language: "唯一利器、金融防火墙、彻底封锁、完美展现"
- Overconfident risk claims not backed by testing
- Treating market-size reports as proof of one startup's demand
- Treating theoretical models as final proof without physical testing

Keep:

- Investment judgment
- Risk categories
- Legal and financial terms
- Cautious but direct recommendation

Prefer:

- Evidence chain: source -> measured fact -> inference -> confidence level
- "有条件推进/暂缓/拒绝" with milestones and gates
- Separate facts, assumptions, and investor judgment

## Crowdfunding / Product Validation

Hard remove:

- "成功验证市场需求" from pledge data alone
- "极低获客成本" unless spend and channel data are available
- "产品有效" unless test or usage data support it
- "大众市场接受" from early adopter data

Keep:

- Platform names
- Pledged amount and backer count
- Launch timing and source attribution

Prefer:

- "早期支持者愿意提前付费"
- "获得初期曝光"
- "后续仍需验证交付、留存、复购和主流渠道获客"

## Academic Summary

Hard remove:

- Promotional significance claims
- Unsupported "填补空白、具有重要意义"
- Casual first-person commentary

Keep:

- Technical terms
- Cautious wording
- Some nominalization where precision requires it

Prefer:

- Research question, method, sample, result, limitation
- Specific citation or source when claims depend on prior work

## Official-Style Draft / Speech

Do not over-clean into casual prose. This genre may require parallelism, value framing, and formal transitions.

Hard remove:

- Redundant slogan stacking
- Hollow "进一步、持续、不断" chains
- Repeated section endings that say the same thing

Keep:

- Required political or institutional framing
- Formal cadence
- Clear hierarchy

Prefer:

- Fewer, stronger parallel phrases
- Concrete policy, unit, date, action, target

## Legal / Contract

Use minimal intervention.

Hard remove:

- Chatbot residue
- Duplicated explanatory fluff
- Non-binding promotional language accidentally mixed into legal text

Keep:

- Defined terms
- Repeated nouns where ambiguity would increase
- Long clauses when they protect precision

Do not simplify a legal sentence if simplification changes obligation, scope, condition, exception, or liability.
